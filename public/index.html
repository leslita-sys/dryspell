Data sources

  • Forecast + past week: Open-Meteo, Canadian GEM model blend (HRDPS 2.5 km resolution near-term, regional GEM beyond ~48h). Hourly rain, temp, humidity, wind, and solar radiation per grid cell, refreshed at most every 15 minutes.
  • Model cross-check: European ECMWF precipitation from the same call. Drives the confidence badge.
  • Area knowledge: Squamish Bouldering guide V4: sun aspect, canopy, dries-fast flags, hike times, and GPS for all 73 areas, plus drying notes where the book has them.
  • Wetness + dry-time verdicts: computed here by the DRYSPELL bucket model, not a published feed. Coefficients are v1 estimates.
